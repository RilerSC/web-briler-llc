## Product

**Brand:** BRILER  
**Domain:** briler.net  
**Type:** International B2B technology company

BRILER combines:

- Custom software development
- Business process automation
- Applied artificial intelligence
- Systems integration
- Legacy modernization
- Cloud, infrastructure and databases
- Technology consulting

BRILER is **not** merely a software house, web design agency, AI agency, MSP, or only an external CTO service.

**Core idea:** Understand the business problem → design the appropriate technology solution → implement it → take it to production.

**Tone:** CEO-first. Pain-first. Premium B2B. International. Technically sophisticated. Calm confidence. Not startup hype. Not a generic development agency.

**Audience:** Primary = CEOs, owners, GMs, COOs, business directors. Secondary = CIO/CTO/IT managers and technical leaders. First visual layer must be executive-readable; technical depth appears later.

**Languages:** Spanish and English are first-class. Layouts must survive longer Spanish copy. Do not design only for short English.

**Markets:** One site. Costa Rica, United States, Spain as presence — not separate websites or service structures.

## Three exploration directions (do not merge)

**A — Executive / Architectural:** precision, confidence, structure, enterprise, architecture, clarity, premium consulting, engineering discipline. CEO-appropriate. Avoid bland corporate.

**B — Engineering / Systems:** systems, connections, architecture, integration, data flows, infrastructure. Executive-friendly. Not a cybersecurity dashboard, IDE, crypto, gaming, or hacker UI.

**C — Premium Technology / Spatial:** premium, modern, spatial, depth, material, light, motion, precision. Subtle glass/translucency/refraction as hierarchy support. Do not imitate Apple. Do not glass everything.
